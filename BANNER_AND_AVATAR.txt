BANNER + AVATAR — how to add your own images

banner.jpg   -> the wide banner across the top.
               Recommended size ~1500x500 (3:1 ratio). JPG or PNG.
               Keep important content out of the bottom-LEFT corner —
               the avatar sits there. Until this file exists, a plain
               soft-blue gradient shows instead (no broken image).

headshot.jpg -> the round avatar overlapping the banner.
               Square image, at least 256x256. Until it exists, a "PB"
               monogram circle shows instead.

Just drop files with these exact names into the repo root and they
appear automatically — no HTML editing needed.
