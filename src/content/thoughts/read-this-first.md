You may be wondering, Pranav, why should I read what you think of about a song? And for that I say, you don't. You are 100% free to agree or disagree with anything said on my interpretation. This section was just me reading through the lines, and share how I can relate to those lines. Many times, I do go back to a song after a few years, and I read the lyrics, and I realize that the words were much deeper than what the song sounded like, and often times it hits close to home. You start wondering, wow for x years, I was singing this song without realizing what it meant.

So pick a song. Maybe you'll find a line you've been singing for years without really hearing it.
